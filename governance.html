Governance framework

Our governance
principles.

MIDROC operates under a strong governance framework built on four principles: Accountability, Responsibility, Transparency and Ownership (ARTO). Sustainability is embedded across the Group’s investment and operational decisions.

Meet our leadership ↗
A

Accountability

We answer for our decisions, performance and results.

R

Responsibility

We act responsibly toward our people, communities and the environment.

T

Transparency

We share clear, honest information with stakeholders, investors and regulators.

O

Ownership

Every leader and employee takes ownership of outcomes, as if the business were their own.
