Our story

A long-term partner
in economic development.

Over more than 30 years the Group has grown into one of the country's largest private employers, with operations across Africa, Europe, the Middle East and the United States.

MIDROC Investment Group is a privately held, diversified investment group with a long-term focus on building sustainable, high-impact businesses across strategic sectors of the economy.

The Group operates through 45+ companies in six clusters — Agro-Industry, Manufacturing, Mining, Construction & Real Estate, Hotels & Resorts, and Commerce — contributing to economic development, job creation, and industrial growth.

Six industries, one group.

Our companies work across essential sectors to build a resilient, connected Ethiopian economy.
