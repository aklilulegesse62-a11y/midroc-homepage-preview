MIDROC history · Timeline

Built over generations

From essential industry to a diversified portfolio serving people and businesses across Ethiopia.

1989

Building an industrial foundation

Abijata Shalla Soda Ash starts production, beginning a new chapter in Ethiopia’s industrial development.

1993

Construction joins the Group

MIDROC Construction Ethiopia is established, expanding the Group’s role in building essential infrastructure.

1997

Growth across essential sectors

MIDROC Gold, ELFORA and Ethio Agri-CEFT begin, establishing the Group’s presence in mining and agriculture.

1998

A landmark in hospitality

Sheraton Addis is established, adding a new destination to Ethiopia’s hospitality sector.

Today

A broader, diversified portfolio

Since then, the Group has grown into new fields, including healthcare, recycling, hotels, dairy and poultry, seed production, gold and coal mining, and real estate development.

Building resilient businesses and sustainable value across strategic industries.
